Video Generation and World Models:Benchmarks and Evaluation

Overview

VGBE 2027 focuses on benchmarks and evaluation for video generation and world models. As generative systems evolve from content synthesis toward interactive world simulation, the workshop aims to advance reliable metrics, standardized benchmarks, and reproducible protocols for assessing both model capabilities and practical utility.

Topics span perceptual quality, prompt alignment, physical realism, temporal dynamics, and interactive behavior, alongside task-specific evaluation for robotics, autonomous driving, and embodied AI. A central question is how to connect general-purpose assessments with performance in real-world applications.

The workshop also explores how human-aligned evaluators can provide reliable feedback for reward modeling, reinforcement learning, and iterative model improvement. We aim to bring together researchers from academia and industry to identify critical failure modes and develop rigorous evaluation practices for video generation and world models.

Topics

01

Evaluation metrics & methods

Fine-grained metrics for visual quality, prompt alignment, temporal consistency, physical realism, and human preferences, including interpretable automated evaluators.

02

Datasets & reproducible benchmarks

Diverse evaluation datasets, standardized benchmarks, and reproducible protocols that enable systematic comparison across video generation and world modeling systems.

03

Trustworthy evaluation & reward feedback

Human-aligned evaluation of safety, bias, fairness, and explainability, with reliable feedback for reward modeling, reinforcement learning, and post-training.

04

Video generation models

Text-to-video, image-to-video, video-to-video, and video editing, alongside autoregressive and streaming generation, controllability, and long-form temporal coherence.

05

World models & interactive simulation

Action-conditioned prediction, spatial and physical consistency, long-horizon world dynamics, interactive simulation, and closed-loop agent performance.

06

Applications & downstream utility

Task-specific assessment in robotics, autonomous driving, embodied AI, and low-level vision, with applications in science, medicine, engineering, and education.
